takeaway 6

The importance of creating a design system that would not only have components but also production ready system that would sustain any further iterations.

Documenting every change so as to not only keep track but also sometimes to bring an idea that would be a great implementation in the current sprint. So, the practise of both DRDs and BRDs is now an easy follow.

Each industry has predefined aesthetics. So, building on that causes less friction and sometimes that simplicity makes overwhelming functionalities flow like a piece of cake.

As someone who also did the branding, how certain elements play through into the products and tell the story was a very interesting learning as creating the authenticity and continuity felt very natural.

Inclusivity between age groups and other factors were stories that was documented and considered very immensely whist creating the platform as sometimes unheard opinions bring the overall performance.

Creating an end-to-end platform taught me how AI is a very good helping hand rather than trying to replace someone's job. How the expertise still lies in the was the work is still conducted and how introducing AI features go hand-in-hand.
